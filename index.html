    About

    DROPGENIUS AI

    Smarter Sourcing. Clearer Decisions.

    DropGenius AI is a local-first desktop workspace for organising product research, estimating profitability, drafting listings and evaluating prices when reliable data is available. Everything is stored on your own device.

    What the app does

    • Organises your product research in one place.
    • Calculates estimated profit and margin from the values you enter.
    • Helps you draft product listings and marketing ideas from local templates.
    • Evaluates a product price against your target — with honest confidence levels.
    • Stores everything locally on your device, with no accounts and no tracking.

    How it works

    1. Research a product idea and save your notes.
    2. Estimate costs, price and margin in the Profit Calculator.
    3. Track products, statuses and estimated profit.
    4. Draft listings and marketing ideas from local templates.
    5. Evaluate prices with the Price Advisor when data is available.

    Limitations — please read

    All profit calculations and price recommendations are estimates only. DropGenius AI does not fetch live market data, sales figures, competition metrics, supplier quotes or real-time pricing. Every figure depends entirely on the values you enter.

    Estimates do not include taxes, refunds, currency fluctuations, shipping delays or returns. Payment processing fees can be estimated using the optional fee percentage field in the Profit Calculator, but any fixed per-transaction fees charged by your processor are not included. Historical prices do not guarantee future prices. Nothing in this application constitutes financial, legal or investment advice. Always verify pricing and product information with the original source before making a purchase decision.
